# 📚 English Grammar — Position of Objects

The **position of objects** in a sentence depends on the type of object and the sentence structure.

## 1. Direct Object

The **direct object** typically comes **after the verb** and receives the action directly.

### Structure

> Subject + Verb + Direct Object

### Example

> She ate an apple.

- **Subject:** She
- **Verb:** ate
- **Direct Object:** an apple

The direct object **"an apple"** follows the verb **"ate."**

---

## 2. Indirect Object

The **indirect object** usually comes **before the direct object** when both are present.

### Structure

> Subject + Verb + Indirect Object + Direct Object

### Example

> He gave me a gift.

- **Subject:** He
- **Verb:** gave
- **Indirect Object:** me
- **Direct Object:** a gift

The indirect object **"me"** comes before the direct object **"a gift."**

---

## 3. Object of a Preposition

The **object of a preposition** always follows the preposition.

### Structure

> Preposition + Object of Preposition

### Example

> She sat on the chair.

- **Preposition:** on
- **Object of Preposition:** the chair

The noun phrase **"the chair"** follows the preposition **"on."**

---

## 4. Multiple Objects

A sentence can contain more than one type of object.

### Example

> She gave her friend a book.

- **Subject:** She
- **Verb:** gave
- **Indirect Object:** her friend
- **Direct Object:** a book

### Structure

> Subject + Verb + Indirect Object + Direct Object

---

## 5. Sentence Structure

The position of objects can change depending on the overall sentence structure.

### Example

> In the garden, she planted flowers.

- **Prepositional Phrase:** In the garden
- **Preposition:** in
- **Object of Preposition:** the garden
- **Subject:** she
- **Verb:** planted
- **Direct Object:** flowers

The prepositional phrase **"In the garden"** appears at the beginning of the sentence, but the direct object **"flowers"** still follows the verb.

---

## 📌 Quick Reference

| Object Type | Typical Position | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct Object** | After the verb | She read **a book**. |
| **Indirect Object** | Before the direct object | She gave **me** a book. |
| **Object of Preposition** | After the preposition | She sat **on the chair**. |

## 🧠 Key Rule

Remember:

> **Verb → Direct Object**

> **Verb → Indirect Object → Direct Object**

> **Preposition → Object of Preposition**

Understanding the position of objects helps you construct grammatically correct sentences and identify the role that each element plays in a sentence.
