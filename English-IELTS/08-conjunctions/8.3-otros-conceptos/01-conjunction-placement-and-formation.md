# Conjunction Placement and Formation

**Conjunctions** are words or groups of words that connect **words, phrases, or clauses** within a sentence.

They help establish relationships such as:

- Addition
- Contrast
- Choice
- Cause
- Condition
- Time
- Result
- Alternative

There are three main types of conjunctions:

1. Coordinating conjunctions
2. Subordinating conjunctions
3. Correlative conjunctions

In addition, **conjunctive adverbs** can connect independent clauses and show logical relationships between ideas.

---

# 1. Conjunction Placement

The position of a conjunction depends on the type of conjunction and what it connects.

---

## 1.1 Coordinating Conjunctions

Coordinating conjunctions connect elements of **equal grammatical importance**.

The most common coordinating conjunctions are:

> `and`, `but`, `or`, `nor`, `for`, `yet`, `so`

They are usually placed **between the elements they connect**.
