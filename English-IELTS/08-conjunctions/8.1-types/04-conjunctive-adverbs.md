# Conjunctive Adverbs

**Conjunctive adverbs** are adverbs that connect or relate **independent clauses** by showing the logical relationship between two ideas.

They can express relationships such as:

- Result or consequence
- Contrast
- Addition
- Similarity
- Time
- Sequence
- Concession
- Cause and effect

Unlike coordinating conjunctions, conjunctive adverbs are **adverbs**, not conjunctions.

---

# 1. What Is a Conjunctive Adverb?

A conjunctive adverb connects two closely related ideas and shows **how those ideas are logically connected**.

### Example

> "She studied hard; **therefore**, she passed the exam."

There are two independent clauses:

> "She studied hard."

> "She passed the exam."

The conjunctive adverb:

> `therefore`

shows that the second idea is a **result** of the first.
