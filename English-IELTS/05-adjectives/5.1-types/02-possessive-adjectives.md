# Possessive Adjectives

Possessive adjectives are used to indicate **ownership or possession**. They show the relationship between the noun being modified and the person or thing that possesses it.

## 1. Definition

Possessive adjectives modify nouns to indicate ownership or possession.

> **Possessive adjective + noun**

Examples:

- **My** house
- **Your** car
- **His** book
- **Her** phone
- **Its** color
- **Our** team
- **Their** bags

---

## 2. Types of Possessive Adjectives

| Subject Pronoun | Possessive Adjective | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | **my** | This is **my book**. |
| You | **your** | Is this **your bag**? |
| He | **his** | That is **his car**. |
| She | **her** | This is **her phone**. |
| It | **its** | The dog is wagging **its tail**. |
| We | **our** | This is **our house**. |
| They | **their** | They left **their bags**. |

---

## 3. Possessive Adjectives + Nouns

Possessive adjectives are normally placed **before a noun**.

### Examples:

- **My** computer is new.
- **Your** English is improving.
- **His** brother is a doctor.
- **Her** car is outside.
- The company changed **its** policy.
- **Our** team won the match.
- **Their** project was successful.
