# Compound Adjectives

Compound adjectives are formed by combining two or more words to create a single adjective that describes or modifies a noun. The words are often hyphenated or sometimes written as a single word.

## 1. Definition

A compound adjective combines two or more words to express a specific characteristic of a noun.

- a well-known actress
- a fast-paced movie
- a five-year-old child

## 2. Formation

Compound adjectives can be formed in several ways:

### Adverb + Past Participle
- well-known
- highly-skilled
- well-written

Example:
- She is a well-known actress.

### Adjective + Present Participle
- good-looking
- hard-working
- long-lasting

Example:
- He is a hard-working employee.

### Noun + Present Participle
- time-saving
- money-making
- energy-saving

Example:
- This is a time-saving solution.

### Adjective + Noun
- dark-haired
- short-tempered
- big-hearted

Example:
- He is a short-tempered person.

### Number + Noun
- five-year-old
- two-hour
- ten-minute

Example:
- It was a two-hour meeting.

## 3. Hyphenation

Compound adjectives are often hyphenated when they appear before a noun. The hyphen helps show that the words work together as one adjective.

- a well-written report
- a long-term plan
- a high-speed connection
- a five-year-old child

However, when the expression comes after the noun or linking verb, the hyphen is often not used.

- The report is well written.
- The plan is long term.
- The child is five years old.

## 4. Single-Word Compound Adjectives

Some compound adjectives are written as a single word.

Examples:

- worldwide
- breathtaking
- outspoken
- hardworking

Example:
- The company has a worldwide presence.

## 5. Descriptive Function

Compound adjectives provide more specific information than a single adjective.

Compare:

- a movie
- a fast-paced movie

- a report
- a well-written report

- a connection
- a high-speed connection

## 6. Important Rule

When a compound adjective comes **before a noun**, hyphens are commonly used to show that the words form one unit.

Examples:

- a two-hour meeting
- a high-quality product
- a long-term strategy
- a state-of-the-art system

## 7. More Examples

- a well-known company
- a highly-skilled engineer
- a full-time job
- a part-time employee
- a user-friendly application
- a data-driven solution
- a cost-effective strategy
- a problem-solving approach
- a state-of-the-art system
- a five-year-old building

## Summary

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Adverb + Past Participle | well-known |
| Adjective + Present Participle | hard-working |
| Noun + Present Participle | time-saving |
| Adjective + Noun | short-tempered |
| Number + Noun | five-year-old |
| Multiple words | state-of-the-art |

Compound adjectives allow English speakers to combine several words into one descriptive unit, making sentences more precise and concise.
