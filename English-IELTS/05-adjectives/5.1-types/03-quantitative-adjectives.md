# Quantitative Adjectives

Quantitative adjectives are adjectives that express **quantity or amount**. They provide information about **how many** or **how much** of something there is.

They answer questions such as:

- **How many?** → countable nouns
- **How much?** → uncountable nouns

---

## 1. Definition

A quantitative adjective modifies a noun by indicating its **quantity or amount**.

### Examples:

- **Three** books
- **Many** people
- **Some** water
- **Few** students
- **Much** time

---

## 2. Types of Quantitative Adjectives

Quantitative adjectives can generally be divided into two groups:

1. **Definite quantitative adjectives**
2. **Indefinite quantitative adjectives**

---

## 3. Definite Quantitative Adjectives

Definite quantitative adjectives indicate an **exact number or quantity**.

### Examples:

- one
- two
- three
- four
- five
- ten
- one hundred
- one thousand

### Examples in sentences:

- I have **one book**.
- There are **two apples** on the table.
- She has **three brothers**.
- We need **five chairs**.
- The company hired **ten employees**.
- The city has **one thousand residents**.
