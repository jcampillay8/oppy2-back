# Emphasizing Adjectives

Emphasizing adjectives, also known as intensifying adjectives, are used to express a stronger degree or intensity of a quality or characteristic. They add emphasis and impact to a description.

## 1. Definition

Emphasizing adjectives strengthen the meaning of a quality or characteristic.

Examples:

- very intelligent
- extremely talented
- incredibly difficult
- absolutely amazing

Example:
- She is extremely talented.

## 2. Purpose

They are used to make a description stronger or more expressive.

Compare:

- He is talented.
- He is extremely talented.

- The movie was good.
- The movie was incredibly good.

## 3. Common Emphasizing Words

Some common intensifying words include:

- very
- extremely
- incredibly
- absolutely
- exceptionally
- remarkably
- particularly
- highly
- truly
- quite

Examples:

- She is very intelligent.
- He is extremely fast.
- It was an incredibly difficult exam.
- That is an absolutely amazing result.
- She is exceptionally talented.

## 4. Position

These words are usually placed before the adjective they intensify.

Structure:

**Subject + linking verb + emphasizing word + adjective**

Examples:

- He is very intelligent.
- She is extremely confident.
- The system is highly reliable.
- The solution is remarkably effective.

## 5. Different Degrees of Intensity

Different words can express different levels of emphasis.

### Moderate emphasis

- quite
- fairly
- rather

Examples:

- The task is quite difficult.
- The system is fairly reliable.

### Strong emphasis

- very
- highly
- particularly

Examples:

- She is very experienced.
- This is a highly effective method.

### Very strong emphasis

- extremely
- incredibly
- absolutely
- exceptionally

Examples:

- The problem is extremely complex.
- The results were incredibly impressive.
- It was absolutely incredible.
- She is exceptionally talented.

## 6. Important Grammar Note

Although these words are sometimes called "emphasizing adjectives," in traditional English grammar, words such as **very, extremely, incredibly, absolutely,** and **quite** are generally classified as **adverbs of degree** because they modify adjectives.

For example:

- very + intelligent
- extremely + difficult
- incredibly + useful

Here, "very," "extremely," and "incredibly" are adverbs, while "intelligent," "difficult," and "useful" are adjectives.

## Summary

| Emphasizing Word | Example |
|---|---|
| very | very important |
| quite | quite difficult |
| highly | highly effective |
| extremely | extremely useful |
| incredibly | incredibly fast |
| absolutely | absolutely amazing |
| exceptionally | exceptionally talented |
| remarkably | remarkably successful |

Emphasizing words make descriptions stronger by increasing the intensity of an adjective. They are especially useful when you want to communicate a strong degree of a particular quality.
