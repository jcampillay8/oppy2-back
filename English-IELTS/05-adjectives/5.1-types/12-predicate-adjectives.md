# Predicate Adjectives

Predicate adjectives, also called predicative adjectives, are adjectives that come after a linking verb and describe or modify the subject of a sentence.

## 1. Definition

A predicate adjective provides information about the subject by describing its quality, condition, or state.

Example:

- She is happy.

In this sentence:

- Subject: She
- Linking verb: is
- Predicate adjective: happy

## 2. Structure

The basic structure is:

**Subject + Linking Verb + Predicate Adjective**

Examples:

- He is tired.
- She looks happy.
- The food smells delicious.
- They seem confident.

## 3. Linking Verbs

Predicate adjectives are commonly used with linking verbs such as:

- be
- become
- seem
- appear
- feel
- look
- sound
- smell
- taste

Examples:

- She is intelligent.
- He became angry.
- They seem nervous.
- The child appears tired.
- I feel happy.
- She looks beautiful.
- That sounds interesting.
- The flowers smell wonderful.
- The cake tastes delicious.

## 4. Description of the Subject

A predicate adjective describes the subject rather than receiving the action of the verb.

Example:

- The movie was entertaining.

Here, "entertaining" describes the movie.

Another example:

- The engineer seems confident.

Here, "confident" describes the engineer.

## 5. Predicate Adjective vs. Adjective Before a Noun

An adjective can appear before a noun or after a linking verb.

### Before the noun

- She is a happy person.

"Happy" describes the noun "person."

### After a linking verb

- She is happy.

"Happy" is a predicate adjective describing the subject "she."

## 6. No Agreement

Predicate adjectives do not change according to the number or gender of the subject.

Examples:

- She is happy.
- He is happy.
- They are happy.
- The students are happy.

The adjective "happy" remains unchanged.

## 7. Predicate Adjective vs. Direct Object

A predicate adjective does not receive the action of a verb. Instead, it describes the subject after a linking verb.

Compare:

- She is tired.
  - "tired" = predicate adjective

- She kicked the ball.
  - "the ball" = direct object

In the first sentence, "is" connects "she" with the description "tired."

## 8. Subject Complement

A predicate adjective is a type of **subject complement** because it completes the meaning of the sentence by describing the subject.

Examples:

- The weather is beautiful.
- He seems nervous.
- The system is reliable.
- The results were impressive.

In each sentence, the adjective provides information about the subject.

## Summary

| Sentence | Subject | Linking Verb | Predicate Adjective |
|---|---|---|---|
| She is happy. | She | is | happy |
| He looks tired. | He | looks | tired |
| The food smells delicious. | The food | smells | delicious |
| They seem confident. | They | seem | confident |
| The system is reliable. | The system | is | reliable |

Predicate adjectives follow linking verbs and describe the subject. They are an important type of **subject complement** and should not be confused with direct objects.
