# Interrogative Adjectives

Interrogative adjectives are words used to **ask questions about a noun**. They modify the noun by requesting specific information about it.

The two main interrogative adjectives in English are:

- **Which**
- **What**

> **Important:** Interrogative adjectives are placed **before a noun**.

---

## 1. Definition

An interrogative adjective modifies a noun and is used to form a question.

### Examples

- **Which book** do you want?
- **What color** is your car?
- **Which restaurant** did you choose?
- **What time** is the meeting?

### Basic structure

**Interrogative Adjective + Noun + Verb + ...?**

Example:

**Which + book + did + you + choose?**

---

## 2. "Which"

**Which** is used when choosing between a **limited or specific set of options**.

### Examples

- **Which book** did you choose?
- **Which movie** do you want to watch?
- **Which color** do you prefer, red or blue?
- **Which car** belongs to you?
- **Which restaurant** should we visit?

### Key idea

**WHICH = Which option?**

There is usually a known or limited set of possibilities.

### Example

> There are three restaurants nearby. **Which restaurant** do you prefer?

---

## 3. "What"

**What** is generally used when the question is **open-ended** and there is no specific list of options.

### Examples

- **What book** are you reading?
- **What color** is your car?
- **What type of music** do you like?
- **What time** is the meeting?
- **What food** do you like?

### Key idea

**WHAT = What information?**

The possible answers are generally open.

### Example

> **What music** do you like?

The person can answer with many possibilities.

---

## 4. "Which" vs. "What"

| Which | What |
|---|---|
| Limited or specific choices | Open-ended question |
| Asks someone to select | Asks for information |
| **Which car** do you want? | **What car** do you drive? |
| **Which color**, red or blue? | **What color** is your car? |
| **Which movie** should we watch? | **What movie** do you like? |

### Compare

**Which shirt do you want?**

There are specific shirts to choose from.

**What shirt do you usually wear?**

The question is more open-ended.

---

## 5. Placement

Interrogative adjectives are placed **directly before the noun** they modify.

### Examples

- **Which book** are you reading?
- **What car** does he drive?
- **Which student** answered the question?
- **What time** does the class start?
- **Which company** do you work for?

### Structure

**Which/What + Noun**

Examples:

- which + book
- what + time
- which + company
- what + color

---

## 6. Interrogative Adjective vs. Interrogative Pronoun

This distinction is important.

### Interrogative Adjective

It is followed by a **noun**.

> **Which book** do you want?

> **What color** do you prefer?

Here:

- **which** → modifies "book"
- **what** → modifies "color"

### Interrogative Pronoun

It **replaces the noun** and stands alone.

> **Which** do you want?

> **What** do you want?

Compare:

**Which book do you want?**

↓

**Which do you want?**

In the first sentence, **which** functions as an interrogative adjective/determiner.

In the second sentence, **which** functions as an interrogative pronoun.

---

## 7. Agreement

**Which** and **what** do not change according to the number or gender of the noun.

### Singular

- **Which book?**
- **What car?**

### Plural

- **Which books?**
- **What cars?**

The interrogative word remains unchanged.

| **What** | **What language** do you speak? |

---

## 9. Questions with "Which"

Use **which** when selecting from known or limited possibilities.

Examples:

- **Which laptop** do you want?
- **Which candidate** was selected?
- **Which option** is correct?
- **Which course** are you taking?
- **Which city** would you like to visit?

---

## 10. Questions with "What"

Use **what** for more general or open-ended information.

Examples:

- **What language** do you speak?
- **What job** do you have?
- **What kind of music** do you like?
- **What time** does the store open?
- **What subject** are you studying?

---

## 11. Quick Identification

To identify an interrogative adjective, look for:

1. A question.
2. **Which** or **what**.
3. A noun immediately following it.

### Example

> **Which computer** do you use?

Ask:

> What word modifies the noun "computer"?

Answer:

> **Which**

Therefore, **which** is the interrogative adjective.

---

## Summary

| Concept | Explanation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Interrogative adjective** | Modifies a noun in a question | **Which book**? |
| **Which** | Used for limited/specific choices | **Which car** do you want? |
| **What** | Used for open-ended information | **What car** do you drive? |
| **Position** | Comes before the noun | **What time**? |
| **Agreement** | Does not change for singular/plural | **Which book / Which books** |
| **Interrogative pronoun** | Replaces the noun | **Which** do you want? |

---

## Quick Rule

**WHICH + NOUN → limited or specific choices**

**WHAT + NOUN → open-ended information**

### Examples

- **Which movie** do you want to watch?
  - Choosing from specific movies.

- **What movie** do you like?
  - Asking for general information.
