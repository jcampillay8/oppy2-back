# Simple Past vs. Past Continuous vs. Past Perfect vs. Past Perfect Continuous

## 1. Simple Past Tense

**Focus:** Completed actions or events in the past.

**Structure:**

Subject + past tense verb

**Example:**

I finished my work yesterday.

**Key idea:** The action **happened and finished in the past**.

---

## 2. Past Continuous Tense

**Focus:** An action that was in progress at a specific time in the past.

**Structure:**

Subject + was/were + verb-ing

**Example:**

She was studying when I called her.

**Key idea:** The action **was happening at that moment in the past**.

---

## 3. Past Perfect Tense

**Focus:** An action that was completed **before another past action or point in time**.

**Structure:**

Subject + had + past participle

**Example:**

They had already left when I arrived.

**Key idea:** One past action **happened before another past action**.

---

## 4. Past Perfect Continuous Tense

**Focus:** An action that was ongoing for a period of time before another past action or point in time.

**Structure:**

Subject + had been + verb-ing

**Example:**

He had been working all day before he took a break.

**Key idea:** Emphasizes **duration or continuity before another past event**.

---

# Key Differences

| | **Simple Past** | **Past Continuous** | **Past Perfect** | **Past Perfect Continuous** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Main focus** | Completed action | Action in progress | Completed action before another past action | Duration of an ongoing action before another past action |
| **Time reference** | Past | Specific moment in the past | Earlier than another past event | Ongoing before another past event |
| **Structure** | Past verb | was/were + verb-ing | had + past participle | had been + verb-ing |
| **Example** | I worked yesterday. | I was working at 8 PM. | I had worked before she arrived. | I had been working for two hours before she arrived. |

# 1. Completion vs. Continuity

### Simple Past

**Completed action**

> I worked yesterday.

The action is finished.

### Past Continuous

**Action in progress**

> I was working at 8 PM.

The action was happening at a specific moment.

### Past Perfect

**Completed action before another past action**

> I had worked before she arrived.

The working happened first.

### Past Perfect Continuous

**Ongoing action before another past action**

> I had been working for two hours before she arrived.

The focus is on the **duration of the activity**.
