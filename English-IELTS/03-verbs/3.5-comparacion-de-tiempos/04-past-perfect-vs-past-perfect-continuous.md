# Past Perfect vs. Past Perfect Continuous

## Past Perfect Tense

**Focus:** An action or event that was completed before another past action or a specific point in the past.

**Structure:**

Subject + had + past participle

**Examples:**

- I had already finished my work when she called.
- They had traveled to Europe before they got married.
- She had studied French for two years before she moved to Paris.

**Key idea:** Focus on **what had been completed before another past event**.

---

## Past Perfect Continuous Tense

**Focus:** An ongoing action or state that was in progress before another past action or a specific point in the past.

**Structure:**

Subject + had been + verb-ing

**Examples:**

- He had been working all day before he took a break.
- They had been waiting in line for hours before the concert started.
- We had been living in the city for five years before we decided to move.

**Key idea:** Focus on **how long an action had been happening before another past event**.

---

# Key Differences

| | **Past Perfect** | **Past Perfect Continuous** |
|---|---|---|
| **Main focus** | Completion / result | Duration / continuity |
| **Action** | Completed before another past event | Ongoing before another past event |
| **Emphasis** | What had happened or been completed | How long something had been happening |
| **Structure** | had + past participle | had been + verb-ing |
| **Example** | I had worked for three hours. | I had been working for three hours. |

# Compare the Meaning

### Past Perfect

> I had cleaned the house before the guests arrived.

**Focus:** The **result**.

The house was clean when the guests arrived.

### Past Perfect Continuous

> I had been cleaning the house for three hours before the guests arrived.

**Focus:** The **activity and duration**.

The emphasis is on the process of cleaning and how long it had been happening.

---

# Another Example

### Past Perfect

> She had written five reports before the meeting.

**Focus:** The number of completed reports.

### Past Perfect Continuous

> She had been writing reports for five hours before the meeting.

**Focus:** The activity and its duration.
