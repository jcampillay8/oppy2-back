# Comparison: Present Tenses

Here's a side-by-side comparison of the **Simple Present, Present Continuous, Present Perfect, and Present Perfect Continuous** tenses.

## 1. Simple Present Tense

**Use:** General truths, habits, routines, repeated actions, and permanent situations.

**Structure:**

Subject + base verb

Example:

- I eat breakfast every morning.
- She works at a bank.

**Key idea:** Something that happens **regularly or generally**.

---

## 2. Present Continuous Tense

**Use:** Actions happening **right now** or around the current time. It can also describe temporary situations.

**Structure:**

Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing

Example:

- I am studying for my exam right now.
- They are playing soccer.

**Key idea:** Something that is **happening now or around now**.

---

## 3. Present Perfect Tense

**Use:** Actions or states that happened in the past but have a **connection to the present**.

**Structure:**

Subject + have/has + past participle

Example:

- I have visited that city before.
- She has finished her work.

**Key idea:** Something **has happened**, and it is relevant to the present.

---

## 4. Present Perfect Continuous Tense

**Use:** Ongoing actions or activities that started in the past and continue into the present. It emphasizes **duration or continuity**.

**Structure:**

Subject + have/has + been + verb-ing

Example:

- She has been studying for three hours.
- They have been working on the project for weeks.

**Key idea:** Something **started in the past and has been continuing**.

---

# Side-by-Side Comparison

| Tense | Main Use | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple Present | Habits, routines, facts | Subject + verb | I work every day. |
| Present Continuous | Actions happening now | Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing | I am working now. |
| Present Perfect | Past action with present relevance | Subject + have/has + past participle | I have worked here before. |
| Present Perfect Continuous | Action continuing from past to present | Subject + have/has + been + verb-ing | I have been working for three hours. |

# Key Differences

### Simple Present

**Habit / routine / fact**

> I work every day.

The action happens regularly.

### Present Continuous

**Action happening now**

> I am working right now.

The action is in progress.

### Present Perfect

**Past action + present connection**

> I have worked here for five years.

The action or situation started in the past and has a connection to the present.

### Present Perfect Continuous

**Duration + ongoing action**

> I have been working for five hours.

The emphasis is on the **activity and its duration**.

# Easy Way to Remember

- **Simple Present →** I work.
- **Present Continuous →** I am working.
- **Present Perfect →** I have worked.
- **Present Perfect Continuous →** I have been working.

### Main Question

| Tense | Think about... |
|---|---|
| Simple Present | **How often? / What usually happens?** |
| Present Continuous | **What is happening now?** |
| Present Perfect | **What has happened?** |
| Present Perfect Continuous | **How long has it been happening?** |
