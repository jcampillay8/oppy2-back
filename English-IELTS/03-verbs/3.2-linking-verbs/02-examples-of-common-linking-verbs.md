# Common Linking Verbs

Los **linking verbs** conectan el sujeto de una oración con un complemento que lo describe, identifica o indica su estado. No expresan una acción directa, sino una **condición, característica, apariencia o estado**.

## 1. Be

Se utiliza para expresar estados, características, identidades o ubicaciones.

- "She is intelligent."
- "They are friends."
- "I am tired."
- "The book was interesting."
- "We will be at the party."

## 2. Seem

Se utiliza para expresar una apariencia o impresión.

- "He seems confident."
- "The movie seems boring."
- "They seem happy together."
- "It seems like a good idea."
- "She seems tired."

## 3. Become

Se utiliza para expresar un cambio de estado o condición.

- "She became a doctor."
- "He became famous overnight."
- "They became close friends."
- "It will become clear soon."
- "We have become better at playing the guitar."

## 4. Look

Se utiliza para describir la apariencia de alguien o algo.

- "He looks tired."
- "She looks stunning in that dress."
- "They look happy together."
- "It looks like rain."
- "The house looks beautiful."

## 5. Feel

Se utiliza para expresar un estado físico o emocional.

- "I feel happy."
- "She feels sad about the news."
- "They feel excited about the trip."
- "He feels nervous before exams."
- "The fabric feels soft."

## 6. Sound

Se utiliza para describir cómo algo parece a través del sonido.

- "The music sounds soothing."
- "Her voice sounds melodious."
- "It sounds like a great plan."
- "The alarm sounds annoying."
- "The car engine sounds strange."

## 7. Remain

Se utiliza para expresar que un estado o condición continúa sin cambiar.

- "He remains calm under pressure."
- "She remained silent throughout the meeting."
- "They will remain friends forever."
- "The issue remains unresolved."
- "We have remained committed to our goals."

## 8. Grow

Se utiliza como linking verb cuando expresa un cambio gradual de estado o condición.

- "The plants grow quickly in the sunlight."
- "He grew taller over the summer."
- "Their friendship grew stronger over time."
- "The company grew rapidly in the market."
- "We have grown fond of each other."

## Key Point

Los **linking verbs** conectan el sujeto con un complemento que describe o identifica al sujeto.

**Subject + Linking Verb + Complement**

> She **is** intelligent.

- **Subject:** She
- **Linking Verb:** is
- **Complement:** intelligent

A diferencia de los **action verbs**, los linking verbs no describen directamente una acción, sino un **estado, característica, apariencia o condición**.
