# Verb "To Be"

The verb **"to be"** is one of the most important and commonly used verbs in English. It can function as a **main verb** or as an **auxiliary (helping) verb**.

## 1. Present Tense

In the present tense, "to be" has three forms:

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I am happy. |
| He / She / It | is | She is a doctor. |
| You / We / They | are | They are students. |

### Examples

- I am tired.
- He is a teacher.
- She is happy.
- It is cold.
- We are ready.
- They are students.

---

## 2. Past Tense

In the past tense, "to be" has two forms:

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I / He / She / It | was | She was tired. |
| You / We / They | were | They were happy. |

### Examples

- I was at home.
- He was tired.
- She was a student.
- We were busy.
- They were at the party.

---

## 3. Future Tense

The future form of "to be" is:

**will be + complement**

"Will be" can be used with all subjects.

### Examples

- I will be there tomorrow.
- She will be happy.
- He will be a great engineer.
- We will be ready soon.
- They will be at home tonight.

---

## 4. "To Be" as a Main Verb

As a main verb, "to be" can describe:

- Identity
- Characteristics
- Feelings or states
- Location
- Occupations

### Examples

- I am an engineer.
- She is intelligent.
- He is tired.
- We are at home.
- They are students.

---

## 5. "To Be" as an Auxiliary Verb

"To be" is also used as a **helping verb** to form different grammatical structures.

### Present Continuous

**am / is / are + verb-ing**

- I am studying.
- She is working.
- They are playing.

### Past Continuous

**was / were + verb-ing**

- I was studying.
- He was working.
- They were playing.

---

## 6. "To Be" in the Passive Voice

"To be" is used as an auxiliary verb to form the **passive voice**.

### Present Passive

**am / is / are + past participle**

- The report is written by the analyst.
- The documents are reviewed every day.

### Past Passive

**was / were + past participle**

- The report was written yesterday.
- The documents were reviewed by the team.

### Future Passive

**will be + past participle**

- The report will be completed tomorrow.
- The project will be finished next week.

---

## 7. Negative Forms

To make negative sentences, add **not** after the verb "to be".

### Present

- I am not tired.
- She is not at home.
- They are not ready.

### Past

- He was not available.
- We were not late.

### Future

- She will not be here tomorrow.
- They will not be ready.

### Common Contractions

| Full Form | Contraction |
|---|---|
| am not | I'm not |
| is not | isn't |
| are not | aren't |
| was not | wasn't |
| were not | weren't |
| will not be | won't be |

---

## 8. Questions

To form questions, place the verb "to be" before the subject.

### Present

- Am I late?
- Is she your teacher?
- Are they ready?

### Past

- Was he at work?
- Were you tired?

### Future

- Will you be there?
- Will they be ready tomorrow?

---

## Key Summary

| Tense | Forms of "To Be" | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present | am / is / are | She is happy. |
| Past | was / were | She was happy. |
| Future | will be | She will be happy. |
| Present Continuous | am/is/are + -ing | She is working. |
| Past Continuous | was/were + -ing | She was working. |
| Passive Voice | be + past participle | The work is completed. |

### Key Idea

**"To be" can work in two main ways:**

1. **Main verb:** describes a state, identity, characteristic, or location.
   - *She is a doctor.*
   - *They are tired.*

2. **Auxiliary verb:** helps form grammatical structures such as continuous tenses and the passive voice.
   - *She is working.*
   - *The report was completed.*
