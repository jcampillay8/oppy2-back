# Verb Phrase

A **verb phrase** is a group of words that contains a **main verb** and, when necessary, one or more **auxiliary (helping) verbs**.

The verb phrase functions as a single unit and expresses the **action, event, or state** of the subject.

## 1. Components of a Verb Phrase

### Main Verb

The **main verb** is the central verb. It expresses the main action or state.

- She **runs** every morning.
- They **study** English.
- He **works** at a company.

### Auxiliary Verbs

Auxiliary verbs help the main verb express:

- Tense
- Aspect
- Voice
- Mood
- Modality

Common auxiliary verbs include:

- **be**
- **have**
- **do**
- **will**
- **can**
- **could**
- **may**
- **might**
- **must**
- **should**
- **would**

### Examples

- She **is studying**.
  - Auxiliary: **is**
  - Main verb: **studying**

- They **have been working**.
  - Auxiliary: **have**
  - Auxiliary: **been**
  - Main verb: **working**

- He **will have finished**.
  - Auxiliary: **will**
  - Auxiliary: **have**
  - Main verb: **finished**

# 3. Verb Phrases and Tenses

Verb phrases are essential for creating different verb tenses.

### Simple Present

**Subject + base verb**

> She **works** every day.

### Present Continuous

**Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing**

> She **is working** now.

### Present Perfect

**Subject + have/has + past participle**

> She **has worked** here before.

### Present Perfect Continuous

**Subject + have/has + been + verb-ing**

> She **has been working** all morning.

### Simple Past

**Subject + past verb**

> She **worked** yesterday.

### Past Continuous

**Subject + was/were + verb-ing**

> She **was working** when I called.

### Past Perfect

**Subject + had + past participle**

> She **had worked** there before moving.

### Past Perfect Continuous

**Subject + had + been + verb-ing**

> She **had been working** for three hours.

### Simple Future

**Subject + will + base verb**

> She **will work** tomorrow.

### Future Continuous

**Subject + will + be + verb-ing**

> She **will be working** tomorrow morning.

### Future Perfect

**Subject + will + have + past participle**

> She **will have finished** the project by Friday.

### Future Perfect Continuous

**Subject + will + have + been + verb-ing**

> She **will have been working** for five hours by noon.

# 4. Verb Phrases with Modal Verbs

Modal verbs can also form part of a verb phrase.

### Examples

- She **can swim**.
- He **should study**.
- They **must leave**.
- We **could help**.
- I **might go**.

Structure:

**Subject + modal + base verb**

> She **can speak** English.

The modal verb modifies the meaning of the main verb.

---

# 5. Verb Phrases and Passive Voice

Verb phrases are also used to form the **passive voice**.

### Active Voice

> The company **built** the system.

### Passive Voice

> The system **was built** by the company.

The verb phrase is:

**was built**

- Auxiliary: **was**
- Main verb: **built**

Another example:

> The project **has been completed**.

- Auxiliary: **has**
- Auxiliary: **been**
- Main verb: **completed**

---

# 6. Verb Phrase vs. Main Verb

It is important to distinguish between the **main verb** and the **entire verb phrase**.

Example:

> She **has been studying** English.

- Main verb → **studying**
- Auxiliary verbs → **has, been**
- Complete verb phrase → **has been studying**

Another example:

> They **will have finished** the project.

- Main verb → **finished**
- Auxiliary verbs → **will, have**
- Complete verb phrase → **will have finished**

## Easy Rule to Remember

> **Verb Phrase = Main Verb + Auxiliary Verb(s), when needed**

Examples:

- **works** → main verb only
- **is working** → auxiliary + main verb
- **has finished** → auxiliary + main verb
- **has been working** → auxiliary + auxiliary + main verb
- **will have been working** → auxiliary + auxiliary + auxiliary + main verb

The **last/main verb** carries the core meaning, while the auxiliary verbs provide grammatical information such as **tense, aspect, voice, mood, or modality**.
