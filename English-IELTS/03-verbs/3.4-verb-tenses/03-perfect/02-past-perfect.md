# Past Perfect Tense

The **Past Perfect Tense** is used to describe an action that happened **before another action or specific point in the past**.

It helps us establish the **chronological order** of two past events.

## 1. Formation of the Past Perfect Tense

The Past Perfect is formed with:

**Subject + had + past participle**

The auxiliary verb **had** is used with **all subjects**.

| Subject | Auxiliary | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | had | I had finished |
| You | had | You had finished |
| He / She / It | had | She had finished |
| We / They | had | They had finished |

### Examples

- She **had finished** her homework.
- He **had already left**.
- They **had visited** the city before.
- I **had eaten** before the meeting.

> **Note:** The main verb must be in the **past participle** form.

---

## 2. Usage of the Past Perfect Tense

### A. An action that happened before another past action

The Past Perfect shows which action happened **first**.

- He **had already left** when I arrived.
- She **had finished** her work before the meeting started.
- They **had eaten** dinner before they went to the cinema.
