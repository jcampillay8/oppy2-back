# Future Perfect Tense

The **Future Perfect Tense** is used to describe an action that **will be completed before a specific point or another action in the future**.

It helps us express that something **will have happened or been completed by a certain time in the future**.

## 1. Formation of the Future Perfect Tense

The Future Perfect is formed with:

**Subject + will + have + past participle**

| Subject | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | will have + past participle | I will have finished |
| You | will have + past participle | You will have finished |
| He / She / It | will have + past participle | She will have finished |
| We / They | will have + past participle | They will have finished |

### Examples

- She **will have finished** her work.
- I **will have graduated** by next year.
- They **will have completed** the project.
- He **will have arrived** by then.

> **Note:** The main verb must be in the **past participle** form.

---

## 2. Usage of the Future Perfect Tense

### A. An action completed before a specific future time

The Future Perfect shows that an action will be finished **by a particular moment in the future**.

- I **will have graduated** by next year.
- She **will have finished** the project by Friday.
- They **will have completed** the course by December.
