# 📚 English Grammar — Complete Study Guide

## 1. Pronouns — Pronombres

### Personal Pronouns

Los pronombres personales reemplazan a personas, animales, cosas o grupos.

| Tipo | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | I, you, he, she, it | we, you, they |
| Object | me, you, him, her, it | us, you, them |
| Possessive Pronouns | mine, yours, his, hers, its | ours, yours, theirs |
| Possessive Determiners | my, your, his, her, its | our, your, their |
| Reflexive | myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself | ourselves, yourselves, themselves |

### Examples

- **Subject:** I am working.
- **Object:** She called me.
- **Possessive:** This book is mine.
- **Possessive Determiner:** This is my book.
- **Reflexive:** He taught himself.

---

## 2. Reflexive Pronouns

Se utilizan cuando el sujeto y el objeto de la acción son la misma persona o cosa.

### Forms

| Person | Reflexive Pronoun |
|---|---|
| I | myself |
| You | yourself |
| He | himself |
| She | herself |
| It | itself |
| We | ourselves |
| You | yourselves |
| They | themselves |

### Examples

- I taught **myself** English.
- She blamed **herself**.
- He prepared **himself**.
- They introduced **themselves**.

---

## 3. Demonstrative Pronouns

Se utilizan para señalar personas, cosas o situaciones.

| Pronoun | Number | Distance |
|---|---|---|
| this | singular | near |
| these | plural | near |
| that | singular | far |
| those | plural | far |

### Examples

- **This** is my book.
- **These** are my keys.
- **That** is my car.
- **Those** are my shoes.

### Rule

- **This / These** → cerca
- **That / Those** → lejos

---

## 4. Interrogative Pronouns

Se utilizan para formular preguntas.

| Pronoun | Use |
|---|---|
| who | personas |
| whom | personas como objeto |
| whose | posesión |
| which | elección |
| what | información |

### Examples

- **Who** is responsible?
- **Whom** did you invite?
- **Whose** car is this?
- **Which** option do you prefer?
- **What** is your name?

---

## 5. Relative Pronouns

Introducen una relative clause y conectan información adicional con un sustantivo.

| Pronoun | Use |
|---|---|
| who | personas |
| whom | personas como objeto |
| whose | posesión |
| which | cosas/animales |
| that | personas o cosas |

### Examples

- The woman **who** lives here is a doctor.
- The person **whom** I met was friendly.
- The student **whose** laptop was stolen reported it.
- The car **which** I bought is new.
- The book **that** I read was interesting.

Noun + Relative Pronoun + Relative Clause
