# Primary Auxiliary Verbs

Los **primary auxiliary verbs**, también conocidos como **primary helping verbs**, son un tipo de auxiliary verb que se utiliza para formar diferentes **tiempos verbales, preguntas, oraciones negativas y la voz pasiva**.

Los tres primary auxiliary verbs son:

- **Be**
- **Have**
- **Do**

Trabajan junto con el **main verb** para expresar información gramatical adicional.

---

## 1. Be

**Be** se utiliza principalmente para:

- Formar **continuous tenses**
- Formar la **passive voice**

### Continuous Tenses

Se combina con el verbo principal terminado en **-ing**.

Examples:

- "She **is studying** for her exams."
  - Present Continuous

- "They **were playing** soccer in the park."
  - Past Continuous

### Passive Voice

Se combina con el **past participle** del verbo principal.

Examples:

- "The book **was written** by him."
  - Past Simple Passive

- "The cake **is being baked** in the oven."
  - Present Continuous Passive

---

## 2. Have

**Have** se utiliza para formar los **perfect tenses**.

Se combina con el **past participle** del verbo principal.

Examples:

- "I **have finished** my work."
  - Present Perfect

- "They **had already left** when I arrived."
  - Past Perfect

- "She **will have completed** the project by tomorrow."
  - Future Perfect

---

## 3. Do

**Do** se utiliza principalmente para formar:

- Preguntas
- Oraciones negativas

Especialmente en **Simple Present** y **Simple Past**.

### Questions

Examples:

- "**Do** you like ice cream?"
  - Present Simple

- "**Did** they go to the party?"
  - Past Simple

### Negative Sentences

Examples:

- "I **do not understand** the instructions."
  - Present Simple

- "They **did not finish** their homework."
  - Past Simple

---

## Summary

| Primary Auxiliary | Main Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Be** | Continuous tenses | She **is studying**. |
| **Be** | Passive voice | The book **was written**. |
| **Have** | Perfect tenses | I **have finished**. |
| **Do** | Questions | **Do** you like it? |
| **Do** | Negative sentences | I **do not understand**. |

## Key Point

Los tres **primary auxiliary verbs** son:

**Be + Have + Do**

Cada uno cumple funciones gramaticales diferentes:

- **Be** → Continuous + Passive
- **Have** → Perfect
- **Do** → Questions + Negatives

Comprender estas tres formas es fundamental para construir correctamente diferentes estructuras verbales en inglés.
