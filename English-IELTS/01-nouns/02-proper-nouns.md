# 📚 Proper Nouns — Sustantivos Propios

> **A proper noun** is a specific name given to a particular person, place, organization, or thing. It is **capitalized regardless of its position in a sentence**. Proper nouns are used to distinguish individual entities from others of the same type.

---

## 1. 👤 Names of People

Proper nouns are used to refer to **specific individuals**.

### Examples

- `John`
- `Mary`
- `Michael Jackson`
- `Elizabeth II`

---

## 2. 🌎 Names of Places

Proper nouns are used to identify **specific locations**.

### Examples

- `London`
- `Paris`
- `Mount Everest`
- `Central Park`

---

## 3. 🏢 Names of Organizations

Proper nouns are used to indicate specific **companies, institutions, or groups**.

### Examples

- `Apple Inc.`
- `United Nations (UN)`
- `Facebook`
- `Greenpeace`

---

## 4. 📜 Names of Historical Events

Proper nouns are used to label **significant historical events**.

### Examples

- `World War II`
- `the Renaissance`
- `the American Revolution`

---

## 5. 📖 Names of Works

Proper nouns can be used to denote the titles of **books, movies, songs, paintings, or other artistic creations**.

### Examples

- `"Pride and Prejudice"` → novel by Jane Austen
- `"The Starry Night"` → painting by Vincent van Gogh
- `"Titanic"` → film
- `"Bohemian Rhapsody"` → song by Queen

---

## 6. 📅 Names of Days, Months, and Holidays

Proper nouns are used for specific **days, months, and holidays**.

### Examples

- `Monday`
- `January`
- `Christmas`
- `Independence Day`

---

# 🔎 Proper Nouns vs. Common Nouns

Proper nouns identify **specific entities**, while common nouns refer to **general categories**.

| Common Noun | Proper Noun |
|---|---|
| `city` | `London` |
| `person` | `John` |
| `river` | `Mississippi River` |
| `company` | `Apple Inc.` |
| `month` | `January` |
| `holiday` | `Christmas` |

### Example

> `river` → **Common noun** because it refers to rivers in general.  
> `Mississippi River` → **Proper noun** because it refers to one specific river.

---

# ⚠️ Important: Capitalization

Proper nouns should be **capitalized consistently**, regardless of where they appear in a sentence.

### Examples

- `I live in **London**.`
- `**Michael** works at the company.`
- `We visited **Mount Everest**.`

This capitalization distinguishes proper nouns from common nouns.

---

# 🧠 Key Takeaways

- **Proper nouns** refer to specific people, places, organizations, things, or events.
- They are **always capitalized** in English.
- They distinguish individual entities from others of the same type.
- They include names of:
  - 👤 People
  - 🌎 Places
  - 🏢 Organizations
  - 📜 Historical events
  - 📖 Works of art
  - 📅 Days, months, and holidays
- Some words can function as either **common nouns or proper nouns**, depending on their usage.

> 💡 **In simple terms:** A **common noun** gives a general name, while a **proper noun** gives the specific name of something.
>
> **Example:** `river` → common noun | `Mississippi River` → proper noun
