# 👥 Collective Nouns — Sustantivos Colectivos

> **A collective noun** is a type of noun that refers to a **group or collection of people, animals, or things as a single unit**. It represents a single entity made up of multiple individuals or objects.
>
> Collective nouns are used to describe a group **acting or being considered as a whole**.

---

## 1. 👥 Grouping of Entities

Collective nouns refer to a collection of individuals, animals, or things that are considered as a **single unit**.

### Examples

- `team`
- `family`
- `flock`
- `herd`
- `crowd`
- `committee`

### Example

> `The team is practicing.`

Here, **team** refers to several people, but they are treated as one group.

---

## 2. 🔤 Singular Form

Collective nouns are typically treated as **singular nouns** in American English, taking a singular verb and singular pronoun when the group is considered as one unit.

### Example

> `The team is practicing.`  
> `It has its own strategy.`

- `team` → collective noun
- `is` → singular verb
- `it` → singular pronoun

> 💡 **Note:** In British English, some collective nouns can take plural verbs when the speaker is emphasizing the individual members of the group.

---

## 3. 🎯 Collective Identity

Collective nouns emphasize the **unity or collective identity** of a group, treating it as a single entity.

### Example

> `The jury reached its verdict.`

Here, **jury** represents several people acting together as one decision-making body.

---

# 4. 👨‍👩‍👧 Groups of People

Collective nouns can represent groups of people in different contexts.

### Examples

- `audience`
- `staff`
- `committee`
- `class`
- `team`
- `family`

### Examples in Sentences

> `The audience was silent.`

> `The committee has reached a decision.`

> `The class is studying English.`

---

# 5. 🐺 Groups of Animals

Some collective nouns describe groups of animals.

### Examples

- `a pack of wolves`
- `a herd of cows`
- `a swarm of bees`
- `a flock of birds`

### Example

> `A flock of birds was flying over the city.`

Here, **flock** represents the group of birds as a single unit.

---

# 6. 🚢 Groups of Things

Collective nouns can also refer to groups of **inanimate objects or things**.

### Examples

- `a collection of books`
- `a fleet of ships`
- `a bunch of flowers`
- `a stack of papers`

### Example

> `A stack of papers was on the desk.`

Here, **stack** represents the papers as a group.

---

# 7. 📝 Modifiers with Collective Nouns

Collective nouns can be modified by **adjectives and determiners**.

### Examples

- `a large crowd`
- `the proud family`
- `an enthusiastic team`

Determiner + Adjective + Collective Noun
